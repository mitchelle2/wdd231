Port Harcourt Chamber of Commerce Site Plan

Site Name

Port Harcourt Chamber of Commerce (PHCC)

The Port Harcourt Chamber of Commerce was selected because it clearly identifies the organization and the community it serves. The website will promote local businesses, encourage economic growth, provide business networking opportunities, and serve as a trusted resource for residents, entrepreneurs, investors, and visitors.

Optional Domain: phchamber.org

Site Purpose

The purpose of this website is to serve as an online resource for businesses and residents in Port Harcourt. Visitors will be able to discover local businesses, learn about community events, view weather information, explore the city, and apply for Chamber membership. The website will also promote local economic development by connecting businesses with customers and potential partners.

Scenarios

  • How can I join the Port Harcourt Chamber of Commerce and what benefits do the different membership levels offer?
  • Where can I find trusted local businesses and organizations in Port Harcourt?
  • What upcoming business events or networking opportunities are available this month?
  • I recently moved to Port Harcourt. Where can I learn more about the city's history, attractions, and local businesses?

Typography

Poppins

Poppins will be used for the website logo, headings, navigation menu, and buttons because it provides a clean, modern, and professional appearance.

Open Sans

Open Sans will be used for paragraphs, forms, tables, and general body text because it is highly readable on both desktop and mobile devices.
